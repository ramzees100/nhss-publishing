For authors

From research to publication.

Our proposed workflow supports careful evaluation, constructive feedback, and clear editorial decisions.

01 / PREPARE

Prepare your manuscript

Select a suitable journal, follow its author instructions, and provide methods, references, and relevant disclosures.

02 / REVIEW

Editorial assessment

Editors screen submissions and arrange expert review where appropriate. Authors respond to feedback and revise their work.

03 / PUBLISH

Build the scholarly record

Accepted manuscripts move through production and publication, with clearly stated access conditions and article information.

Manuscript submissions are not open yet. This website does not currently accept files, create accounts, or collect payments.

About NHSS Publishing

Research can begin anywhere.

NHSS stands for Natural Sciences, Humanities, and Social Sciences.

Our aim is to develop an independent publishing organization welcoming research from universities, institutes, industry, and independent scholars around the world.

Our publishing program, governance, editorial boards, and operational policies are being developed.

Research quality

Evaluate contributions through appropriate expertise, evidence, and editorial scrutiny.

Editorial independence

Keep scholarly acceptance decisions independent of payment and commercial arrangements.

Transparency

Establish clear policies for authorship, conflicts of interest, fees, licensing, and peer review.

A lasting record

Develop procedures for preservation, corrections, and publication histories before launching journals.

Our future community

Scholarship advances through people.

A strong publishing organization brings authors, reviewers, and editors together around a shared responsibility for the research record.
