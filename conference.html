Submission Guidelines

These are planning notes, not an active call for papers. Each event will publish its own final submission instructions.

  • Confirm the event scope, accepted paper types, and deadlines.
  • Follow the event’s formatting, length, and language requirements.
  • Provide author details, an abstract, keywords, and references.
  • Include relevant ethics, funding, and conflict-of-interest disclosures.
  • Follow the event’s originality and simultaneous-submission policies.
  • Check review, presentation, registration, and proceedings requirements.

Possible contribution formats
