NHSS Journal of Natural Sciences

A proposed scholarly journal for research in physics, chemistry, biology, and earth and environmental sciences.

This is a proposed journal page. Submissions are closed, no editorial board has been appointed, and no articles or issues have been published.

Aims and scope

The proposed journal will provide a home for original research and critical synthesis within the natural sciences. Its final scope and publication policies will be established by its editorial leadership before launch.

Physics and astronomy

Physical phenomena, matter, energy, optics, and astronomical research.

Chemistry

Chemical systems, materials, reactions, and analytical methods.

Biology and life sciences

Living systems, biological processes, ecology, and related research.

Earth and environmental sciences

Earth systems, environmental processes, and natural resources.

Proposed article types

Original Research Article

New findings supported by a clear research question, appropriate methods, and evidence.

Review Article

Critical synthesis of existing research on a defined topic.

Short Communication

A focused research contribution presented in a shorter format.

Access and publication fees

The journal’s access model, licensing terms, and any author charges have not yet been decided. These will be published before submissions open.

Editorial Board

Editorial leadership is being developed.

No editor-in-chief, associate editors, or editorial board members have been appointed for this proposed journal.

Information to be published

  • Editors’ names, relevant expertise, and verified affiliations.
  • Editorial responsibilities and conflict-of-interest policies.
  • Official editorial contact information.

Author Guidelines

The following is a preliminary preparation checklist, not final submission instructions. Templates, length limits, review policies, and submission requirements will be confirmed before launch.

  • Provide a clear title, author information, abstract, and keywords.
  • Explain the research question, methods, results, and limitations.
  • Cite relevant sources and identify reused material.
  • Include funding, authorship, and conflict-of-interest disclosures.
  • Provide applicable research ethics approvals and consent information.
  • Describe the availability of supporting data and code where relevant.

Manuscript languages

The website supports Arabic and English. Accepted manuscript languages and translation requirements will be established separately in the journal’s final author guidelines.

Submissions are not open. Do not send manuscripts or payments through this demonstration website.

Issues & Articles

No published issues yet.

Published volumes, issues, and articles will appear here after the journal launches. No publication dates have been set.
